Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

We store only what is needed to run the game: a unique player ID, your chosen username, and gameplay statistics (score, streaks, solved levels). Passwords are salted and hashed — we never store plaintext passwords.

Your data powers progress sync, statistics, achievements, and the global leaderboard. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.
